## About the Lab

**Rational Agents Lab** explores how rational decision-making principles
from economics and game theory can inform the design of intelligent systems.
We study the boundary between *rational optimisation* and *human-aligned
behaviour*—how agents balance goals, trade-offs, and moral constraints.

**Current themes**

- Rationality ↔ bounded rationality in learning systems  
- Emergent cooperation in multi-agent environments  
- Utility maximisation as a framework for selective learning and goal generation
