title: "Rationality and Alignment"
date: 2025-10-22
---

In traditional economics, rationality means consistent preference
ordering and utility maximisation.  
In AI, rationality must also incorporate **alignment**—bounded
optimisation constrained by human values.

This note sketches an ongoing line of thought: can reinforcement-learning
agents develop *bounded rationality* through reward shaping and
curriculum bias?  
Future experiments in the Rational Agents Lab aim to explore this link.
