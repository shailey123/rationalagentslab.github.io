# Rational Agents Lab

**Exploring the logic and limits of intelligent systems**

We study how rational behaviour emerges—and sometimes breaks down—in
learning systems.  
Our focus areas:

- **Utility-theoretic learning:** linking reinforcement learning and consumer theory.  
- **Game-theoretic rationality:** multi-agent interaction, cooperation, equilibrium.  
- **Open-ended intelligence:** curiosity, goal discovery, and evolving rationality.
